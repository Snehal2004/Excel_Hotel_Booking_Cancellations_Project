# 🏨 Hotel Booking Cancellations Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes hotel booking data to understand booking cancellation patterns, customer behavior, and differences between City Hotels and Resort Hotels.

The interactive Excel dashboard helps identify cancellation trends, compare hotel performance, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve booking management and reduce cancellations.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Hotel cancellations can affect revenue, room availability, staffing, and operational planning.

The goal of this project is to answer key business questions:

- What percentage of hotel bookings are canceled?
- Which hotel type experiences more cancellations?
- Which customer segments contribute most to cancellations?
- How do booking lead times relate to cancellations?
- What booking characteristics may help hotels identify cancellation risks?

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains **119,390 hotel booking records** with 36 columns covering booking details, customer information, hotel types, and reservation outcomes.

The data covers bookings associated with arrival years 2015, 2016, and 2017.

Key columns include:

- `hotel` — Type of hotel (City Hotel or Resort Hotel)
- `is_canceled` — Whether the booking was canceled (1 = Yes, 0 = No)
- `lead_time` — Number of days between booking and arrival
- `arrival_date_month` — Month of arrival
- `market_segment` — Customer booking segment
- `customer_type` — Type of customer
- `deposit_type` — Deposit arrangement
- `adr` — Average Daily Rate
- `reservation_status` — Final reservation status


## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project explores:

1. **Cancellation Analysis** — Measures canceled versus non-canceled bookings.
2. **Hotel Type Comparison** — Compares City Hotel and Resort Hotel booking performance.
3. **Customer Segmentation** — Examines booking behavior across customer types and market segments.
4. **Lead-Time Analysis** — Investigates how far in advance customers book and whether cancellation patterns differ.
5. **Booking Characteristics** — Explores deposit types, special requests, and other booking attributes.
6. **Dashboard Reporting** — Presents key findings through Excel-based summaries and visualizations.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, hotels can consider the following actions:

- **Monitor cancellation rates:** Track cancellations by hotel type and customer segment.
- **Review advance bookings:** Investigate whether long lead times are associated with higher cancellation risk.
- **Evaluate deposit policies:** Compare cancellation behavior across deposit types before changing policies.
- **Improve demand forecasting:** Use historical booking and cancellation patterns to support room and staffing plans.
- **Target customer segments:** Develop booking and retention strategies based on observed customer behavior.

These are recommended areas for investigation; specific business actions should be supported by the relevant dashboard findings.
